Orthogonal design - difference in behavior, instead of values:
- Instead of having a gun B, that is just buffed version of gun A, we make the behavior of gun B fundamentally distinct to gun A

This keeps the game interesting, by demanding *variety* from players when dealing with problems.

## R.E.P.O.
Dealing with monsters is fundamentally changes through the game. At the beginning, players can only *hide* from monsters, but later on they unlock weapons, providing an option to *fight* as well.
