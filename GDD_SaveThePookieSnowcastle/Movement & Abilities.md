# Elden Ring

| Primary    | Secondary   | Lock Target View | Movement | Heavy | Special | Support | Interact | Crouch |

- WASD - Movement
- Primary Pressed - Attack
- Secondary Pressed - Secondary Ability

- Special Pressed - Weapon Special
